Good to know

  • Wallet balance expires 6 months after purchase. The oldest balance is used first; the Developers page shows the next expiry date.
  • All sales are final — no refunds. Kaal's answers are astrological guidance for personal reflection only, not medical, legal, financial or other professional advice, and predictions are not guaranteed.
  • Failed deliveries are not charged. If Kaal cannot start or a question cannot be delivered (503 unavailable), the charge is not taken or is returned to your wallet automatically.
  • Requests rejected with a 4xx error (for example invalid_request or answer_pending) cost nothing.
  • All keys on an account share one balance. Check it with GET /v1/balance.
