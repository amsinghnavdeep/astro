Introduction

The Kaalera API lets your product ask Kaal, Kaalera's AI Vedic astrologer, questions about a person's future and get back a written, explained answer.

What Kaal does

When you create a consultation, Kaalera computes the Kundli from the date, time and place of birth, then Kaal reads it and answers the question. Every answer shows its working: the planets, houses, dashas and transits behind it, plus the timing windows that matter.

  • AI trained by a 7,000-year-old lineage of Kashmiri Himalayan monks, reading classical Vedic (sidereal) Jyotish.
  • Planet positions cast to the arc-second for the exact birth time and place.
  • Vimshottari dasha periods and upcoming transits of the slow planets.
  • Answers in nine languages, including Hindi and Hinglish.
i

Astrology is interpretation, not a guarantee. Kaal explains why it says what it says so your users can judge for themselves. Do not present answers as certain predictions.

How it works

  1. Create a consultation

    Send the birth details and a first question to POST /v1/consultations. This costs 1 credit.

  2. Poll for the answer

    Kaal usually answers within a few minutes. Poll GET /v1/consultations/:id until a message with role: "kaal" appears.

  3. Ask follow-ups

    Ask more questions in the same consultation with POST /v1/consultations/:id/questions for 1 credit each. Kaal remembers the Kundli and the earlier answers. Consultations stay open for 7 days.
